Why I started

ANNA ZHANG

I want to make measuring and dispensing liquids easier for people with visual impairments while keeping the solution accessible and affordable.

My motivation

I have seen friends around me, and family members of friends, experience visual impairment and the struggles they face in daily life. Many existing assistive products are expensive or difficult to find on the market, so I want to create a solution for this problem that can be easily assembled and given to visually impaired people.

By making a device that is simple to build and use, I hope to promote their independence and improve their quality of life.

From interviews to a prototype

Through the interviews I conducted with visually impaired members of the Vista Center community, one theme that came up multiple times was the ability to measure and transfer liquids reliably, sometimes not just in cooking.

My automatic voice-controlled liquid dispensing system grew out of that problem. I connected and tested the components, integrated the pump and scale, added buttons and voice recognition, and 3D printed the pump and outer case. Testing led me to change the tubing and add an experimental overshoot-compensation model.
